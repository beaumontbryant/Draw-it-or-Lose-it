# Draw-it-or-Lose-it

1. Briefly summarize The Gaming Room client and their software requirements. Who was the client? What type of software did they want you to design?
The client was a gaming company called "The Gaming Room." They had a successful game called "Draw it or Lose it" that was built for Android OS. They wanted to open up the game to the PC world. I was tasked with accomplishing that goal. 


2. What did you do particularly well in developing this documentation?
I beleive I laid out a solid recommendation. I answered all their questions in terms of requirements. 


3. What about the process of working through a design document did you find helpful when developing the code?
It was useful to have their requirements fully laid out on paper. This allowed us to work within the confines of thier vision to achieve the goal.


4. If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?
I don't think I would change anything explicitly. But I would find more value in walking through the UML Chart. I found it helpful to map the needs and desires visually.


6. How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your software design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing?
You need to understand explicitly what they are asking for. A company asking you to put a game on a PC can mean many different things. Some PCs have app stores these days. Do they want that. Is the browser fine? Is only windows fine? All of these questions can stem from one simple ask.


7. How did you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a similar software application?
I think the UML diagram was epecially useful. Especially if you are working in a team, where not everyone knows all the peices.
